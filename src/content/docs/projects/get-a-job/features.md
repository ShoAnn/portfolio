## Features
- Job Application and Resume/CV Tracking
- Add, view, edit, and archive job applications with role title, company, status, application date, and portal link
- 9 application statuses: `draft`, `sent`, `under_review`, `interview_scheduled`, `offer_extended`, `accepted`, `rejected`, `withdrawn`, `archived`
- List view with search, status filter, and sorting by application date, company, or status
- Detail page with two-column layout — job information on the left, note editor on the right
- Kanban Board. Drag and drop columns grouped by status, releasing after dragging a card will instantly update the job status
- Dashboard. Shows statistics of total saved, total applied, Chart Status details (Recharts), and days since first application
- Authentication & Account:
    - Sign up/sign in with email, username, and password
    - JWT access token in an httpOnly cookie with refresh token rotation
