## Project Overview

**Get a job**, is a job application tracking app that streamlines the job search by centralizing every stage of the hiring process into an organized structure. Designed to replace the current widely used methods which are spreadsheets and note apps, the platform allows candidates to log applications, track status updates from initial submission to final offer, and store tailored resumes and cover letters for every role. Additional integration features such as deadline reminders, interview scheduling, and automated metrics added to further optimize efficiency and effectivity of user's time and effort.
